# endo-label-product/03 — What word names a durable label across task types

## Question

`CONTEXT.md` **Annotation** is the durable pixel masks and metadata for Tracks on a Clip. This product will also persist Phase, Frame class, and Triplet records.

What word is the durable unit **across** task types?

Options the grill should settle (or replace): keep **Annotation** for masks only and pick a new umbrella; broaden **Annotation** to mean any durable Clip label; use **Label** / **Record** / another term.

Update `CONTEXT.md` when the word locks. Do not design storage files here.

## Answer

No umbrella word. The four durable kinds are named **phase**, **class**, **triplet**, **mask**. Glossary keeps **Phase**, **Frame class** (spoken **class**), **Triplet**, **mask**. **Annotation** stays the current service’s disk store of masks — not a name for Phase/class/Triplet. When you must mention all four, list them.
